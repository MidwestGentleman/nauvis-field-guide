About this guide

The Nauvis Field Guide is an unofficial, fan-made guide to AresRPG. It isn't affiliated with, made by, or endorsed by the AresRPG team.

Why it exists

The game has an excellent encyclopedia, but it can be hard to navigate as a new player. This guide is built to help new players find their way around the game.

Accuracy

Data pages are generated from the game's latest release, and the stamp at the bottom of every page says when. Hand-written rules were checked against the game's contracts in October 2026. Player measurements, such as gas costs, are labelled as measurements. If something here disagrees with the game, the game is right.

Credits

AresRPG, its names, content, data and artwork belong to their creators. The pictures of mobs, items, spells, classes and places are the game's own art. Your browser loads them straight from the official repository, at the same release as the data, and none of them are stored in or redistributed by this site.
