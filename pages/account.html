Wallet, login and gas

AresRPG runs on the Sui blockchain, so it works differently from a game you download. You don't need to understand blockchains to play, but a few basics will help.

Most online games are run by a company. The company pays for the servers, keeps your account in its database, and takes the money when you buy items or packs. AresRPG's code is open source, and the game runs on Sui, a public network kept going by many independent computers instead of one company's servers. Every action you take, like a step in a fight, a harvest or a craft, is recorded as a transaction on that network. Each one costs a tiny amount of SUI, called gas. That's why the game isn't free to play.

In return, you own what you earn. Your characters, items and pets live in your own Sui account, not on a company's server. When you sell something on the marketplace, the SUI goes to you, the seller. So the loot you gather and sell can pay for your gas.

Logging in

To start playing, press Continue with Google on the landing page. This creates a new Sui address for you through Sui's secure zkLogin. Similar to "sign in with Google" on other websites, your Google login then controls that personal Sui address, and the game signs your transactions inside the game itself. There are no wallet popups, no extension and no seed phrase. Google is the only way to log in, so you can't log in with a Sui wallet you already have. You can still send SUI from it to your game address.

  • Your characters, items and pets belong to that address. Log in with the same Google account on another computer and they're all there.
  • The address only signs inside the game tab. Other websites can read what you own on chain, but they can't act for you.

Try the free demo first. Before you log in, the game offers a short adventure. You play a level-199 Senshi alongside a companion and fight goblins up to a goblin king. It's off-chain and free, and nothing carries over. It's a good way to learn the fight controls before you spend anything.

What costs gas

Everything that changes the game state is a transaction:

Activity Transactions
Searching a zone (finding what spawned) 1 per search
Gathering 1 per resource node
Fighting 1 to start or join, 1 per placement move, 1 per turn, 1 to collect loot
Crafting 1 per batch. Stackable items batch up to 1,000 attempts; gear is 1 per transaction.
Equipping, eating, using a potion 1 each
Entering a dungeon 1, and the key is burned
Marketplace 1 to list or buy. Each sale also pays a 10% royalty.
Crushing gear into runes, opening a loot box 2 steps each

Failed transactions still cost gas. If one illegal action is in your fight turn, the whole turn is rejected, and you pay the gas anyway.

Why you need $SUI:

  • 1 SUI per character. That's the creation price, and it goes to the game's treasury. Deleting the character does not refund it.
  • Gas. Each action is a small transaction. A single one costs a tiny fraction of a SUI, but a long play session adds up.

As a rough guide, one player measured these costs in October 2026 and reported:

  • a harvest about 0.0005 $SUI
  • a short solo fight about 0.003 $SUI
  • a zone search about 0.003 $SUI
  • a full 8-room Gilded Lorito run about 0.05–0.07 $SUI

Long fights cost more, because every turn is a transaction. Start with 2 $SUI and you have plenty of room.

Don't hoard your inventory. Gear you won't use is better off sold or crushed. List it on the marketplace, or crush it for runes. Every item you own holds a small storage deposit on Sui, and when an item is crushed or a stack is used up, about 99% of that deposit comes back to you. That's why your SUI balance can go up after crushing.

One tab

Play in a single tab. Two game tabs can block each other or kick you out. Browsers also slow down hidden tabs, so walking and in-game automation stall when the game window is covered or minimised. If the page reloads by itself or says game_frozen, the game is being updated. Wait a minute and reload.

Staying safe

  • Only sign transactions inside the game tab on aresrpg.world.
  • Your items live on chain, so nobody can roll them back, including after a scam. Trade through the in-game marketplace or trade window, only do not entertain or take side deals arranged in DMs even on the discord server.
  • Guides and fan tools like this one only read public chain data. None of them should ever ask you to sign anything.
