Stats, elements and damage

These are the formulas the game runs, written out plainly. They explain where the numbers in a fight come from.

Each level gives 5 stat points and 1 spell point. Stat prices rise as a stat grows, and the price steps differ by class; each class page has the chart. Vitality is always 1 point for +1 (Ikari gets +2). Wisdom is always 3 points for +1.

Which element beats which

AresRPG has no fixed rock-paper-scissors between elements. Every mob has its own resistance to each of the four, read from the game's data. The pattern across all mobs is still clear. Mobs resist their own element most, so a fire mob takes the least damage from fire. Most mobs are also weak to one other element, and a negative resistance adds that much extra damage with no cap. Protectors (the resource guardians) have no weakness at all: they only resist their own element harder.

The damage formula

  1. Roll the spell's min–max for that element.
  2. Multiply by (100 + your stat for that element) / 100, then add your flat Damage.
  3. Apply the target's resistance to that element. Positive resistance reduces damage, capped at 50%. Negative resistance increases it, with no cap.
  4. Subtract any shields that match the element (or cover all elements). Damage can go all the way to 0.

Example: a 10–14 fire spell with 100 Intelligence and +5 Damage does 25–33 before resistance.

  • Heals = roll × (100 + Intelligence) / 100.
  • Life steal deals normal damage, and you heal half of what landed.
  • Poison (damage over time) is calculated once when cast, then ticks at the start of each of the target's turns, once for every turn it lasts. Shields reduce each tick, the same way they reduce a direct hit; resistance isn't applied again.
  • Regeneration (healing over time) ticks the same way, at the start of each of the target's turns.
  • Crits are rolled once per spell per turn. If the first cast of a spell crits, every cast of it that turn crits. A crit swaps in the spell's crit effects, and weapon crits deal ×1.5.
  • Weapon strikes hit every target in the weapon's area, allies included. Using your class's weapon type adds +10%.

AP and MP removal

Taking away AP or MP is a contest of Wisdom: it works about half the time against low-Wisdom targets, and gets close to the 10% floor against high-Wisdom ones. Some spells say they can't be dodged, and those always land. Mobs have no Strength, Intelligence, Chance or flat Damage, so lowering those on a mob does nothing. Lowering a mob's Agility or Wisdom does work.

Character XP

The XP curve tops out at level 200. Reaching level 10 takes 19,200 XP, level 20 takes 171,000, level 30 takes 621,600 and level 50 takes 5,350,000. Only winners earn XP. In a party, the mobs' XP is multiplied by 1.0 / 1.1 / 1.5 / 2.3 / 3.1 / 3.6 for 1–6 players, then shared by level and boosted by each player's Wisdom. XP shrinks when your level is far above the mobs'.
