Tournaments

Tournaments are player-versus-player events run on chain. Teams register for an announced event, then play single-elimination matches until one team is left. Prizes are SUI, KARES and items.

Tournaments are new and change often. This page was checked against game release v2.7.10 (November 2026).

How an event is set up

  • Each tournament has a format: teams of 1, 3 or 6 characters.
  • Up to 64 teams can register. Matches are single elimination. With 3 or more teams, the two semifinal losers also play a bronze match for third place.
  • All settings are fixed at creation and can't be edited afterwards: format, scheduled start, entry fee, level limit and prizes.
  • The entry fee is optional and charged per character. Half goes into the prize pool and half to the game's treasury. Registration shows the exact fee before you confirm, and fees aren't refunded, even if you leave the team or the event is cancelled.
  • The maximum registration level is optional. It's checked only when a character registers, so levelling up afterwards doesn't disqualify it.

Registering

  • Create a team, or find one and take an open seat. A team can be open to anyone or restricted to named characters.
  • A character can be registered with only one team at a time.
  • Only complete teams enter. Teams still missing characters at the start are left out.
  • Anyone can contribute SUI or KARES to the prize pool, even without entering. Contributions are final.

Start and matches

  • Each event has a scheduled start. Nobody can start it earlier, admins included. From that time on, anyone can start it if enough teams are complete.
  • The minimum is 2 complete teams, 4 if there are third and fourth place prizes, and 8 if there are fifth place prizes. Below that, the event can only be cancelled.
  • Starting freezes the rosters and prize totals and pairs the teams at random.
  • Each match opens a 60-second admission window. The game tries to enter your registered characters automatically during it, so stay online with some SUI for gas.
  • A team with no opponent advances by bye; a team whose opponent doesn't show up wins by walkover. Walkovers don't award consolation places.

Prizes

  • Each place has its own share of the SUI and KARES pools. A place's share is split across all the registered seats on that team, including absent teammates. Joint fifth places share one allocation.
  • One claim collects all of a character's coin prizes. Item prizes are claimed separately, and their random stats are rolled when they're claimed.
  • There's no platform fee on prizes; normal gas applies.
  • If an event is cancelled, the pool goes back to the original organizer, not to each contributor.

Free practice

From a tournament, with a character selected, you can play a free local 1v1 practice tournament against bots: six entrants, full health each round, and no fees, rewards or saved progress.
