Choosing a class

You pick your class once and keep it forever. Every class starts with the same 55 HP, 6 AP and 3 MP; what differs is the 20 spells it will ever have and which stats it can raise cheaply. Read the spell list before you choose.

No class change, ever. If you want to try another class, make another character (1 SUI). Stat and spell points can be reset with scrolls; the class can't.

How to choose

  1. Pick a playstyle

    Do you want to hit from range (Yogan archer, Iyashi healer), stand in front (Shugo guardian, Senshi warrior), control the board with traps and pushes (Yajin, Tokei), or buff and support a team (Tomoda, Iyashi)? Your first three spells show the style straight away.

  2. Pick your element

    Each damage element is powered by one stat, and you'll put most of your points into it. A class with one main element is simplest to build; a class split across elements gets more flexible later but is harder to gear early.

  3. Check the stat costs

    Each class page charts what +1 costs in each stat. Long green bars are the stats a class is built to raise.

  4. Read the unlock ladder

    Spells unlock at levels 1, 1, 1, 3, 6, 9, 13, 17, 21, 26, 31, 36, 42, 48, 54, 60, 70, 80, 90 and 100. Most of your first month is spent with the first eight or so, so make sure you like those.

  5. Think about your team

    Dungeons are team content. A party of a damage dealer, a tank and a support clears them far more easily than three of the same.
