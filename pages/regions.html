Regional spawn rules

Cities override biomes. Inside a city's 3 × 3 zones, only that city's own mobs and resources spawn, whatever the ground looks like. That one rule explains most "where is it?" questions, starting with Loritos.

The rule

  • Outside a city, a zone spawns the mobs and resources listed for its biome.
  • Inside a city area (the 9 zones around Thebes, The Ruins or Fuwage), the biome is ignored. Only rows tagged with that city spawn.

So a mob tagged "forest + Thebes" lives in every forest on Nauvis and everywhere in the Thebes area. A mob with no biome and only a city tag lives in that city's area and nowhere else.

Who is tied to which city

The Lorito restriction

The four elemental Loritos (Stone, Magma, Nakwa, Gale) have no biome at all. They spawn only in the 9 Thebes zones. Their drops:

Lorito dropUsed for
Key of the Gilded Lorito (3%)Entering the first dungeon
Lorito FeatherStone Lorito Plume, which goes into the crafted Gilded key
Beak ShardScrap Pickaxe
Lorito DownPotion of Thebes
Water (Lorito Nakwa, 70%)Almost every refining recipe

The Golden Lorito never appears in the open world. It's the boss of the Gilded Lorito dungeon.

Inside Thebes there's nothing else. The Thebes area spawns only Nooks, Brambles, Tinkers and Loritos, plus wheat, green mushroom and quartz. Its resource patches are 1.5× bigger than normal. Boars, ants, Misui and barley only spawn outside it.

Where the other dungeon keys come from

  • Tangled Aftermath: every Aragne drops it 2% of the time. Aragnes are tied to The Ruins but also roam their biomes (Fire Aragne fills deserts). The archimob Arakiri drops it 30%.
  • Ivory Rampart: Fuwa and Nifuwa drop it 1% of the time. They're tied to Fuwage but also roam plains, highlands, forests and rainforests. Fukuo drops it 20%.
  • All keys can also be crafted by a Handyman. See Dungeons and keys.

Archimobs and protectors

Two kinds of mob never spawn by these rules: archimobs swap into normal packs 1% of the time, and protectors ambush 2% of gathers. Both are explained in How you meet mobs.
