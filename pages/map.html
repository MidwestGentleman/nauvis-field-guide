Map and biomes

Nauvis is a square world, 100,000 blocks on a side, cut into 512-block zones. Each zone outside a city belongs to one biome, and the biome decides which mobs and resources can spawn in it.

Reading the map

  • The centre is 0, 0. The in-game map puts the Star Gate at the centre of the world. (On chain the same spot is 50,000, 50,000; fan tools sometimes show those numbers.)
  • Zones are 512 × 512 blocks, numbered from the zone that holds the centre (0, 0). Thebes is the 3 × 3 block from 0, −1 to 2, 1. Only the 3 × 3 zones around your selected character are loaded. Each zone has its own roster of mob groups and resource patches, rolled from a seed.
  • Undiscovered zones look empty. Press G inside one to search it (one transaction). After that, the zone can be searched again, which rerolls everything in it, once every 2 hours.
  • Cities cover the 3 × 3 zones around their centre and follow special spawn rules. See regional spawn rules.
  • Distance from the centre means danger. Near the Star Gate, mobs are at the low end of their level range and come alone. Groups grow to 2–4 about 2,000 blocks out and to 6 further away, and levels reach the top of their range around 20,000 blocks out.

Fuwage about −35,800, −27,300 Ivory Rampart Far out; full groups, top-of-range levels

Potions of Thebes, the Ruins and Fuwage teleport you to their city; a Recall Potion takes you back to the Star Gate. None of them work during a harvest, during a protector ambush, or inside a dungeon run. Riding a pet (X) makes you 1.5× faster.

The Ruins is heavy to render. If the game struggles near it, lower the graphics quality and render distance before you arrive.

How each biome looks on the minimap

The minimap colours each spot by what's on the ground there, then shades it so hills catch light from the north-west. These are real minimap views, one per biome, made with the game's own map-drawing code. Each spot was chosen where that biome fills the whole view, away from cities, and the markers are left off. A biome isn't one flat colour: forest has sandy clearings, rainforest runs down to the sea, and the highlands are bare grey rock with snow on the peaks.

Yakutia resources

On Yakutia's minimap almost everything is snow, so tell its biomes apart by what breaks the white: frozen blue water in taiga, frostfen and blue steppe, dark ice across black ice, and black rock in the caldera.
