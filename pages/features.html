Controls and handy features

The main keys, how to travel fast, and how to run several of your own characters together. All of it comes from the game client and contracts.

Getting around fast

  • Click the world map (M) and your character runs there by itself. Position links in chat, party members and fight markers work the same way. Any movement key stops the auto-run.
  • Ride your pet with X for 1.5× speed. The bonus only counts on a trip you started with the pet equipped.
  • City potions (, , ) teleport you to that city. They only work for a city in the world you're in.
  • sends you to your world's centre, where the Star Gate stands.
  • The Star Gate (T near the world centre) moves you between worlds, if you meet the destination's level. You arrive at that world's gate, and each character travels on its own.

When travel items don't work. Not in a fight, not inside a dungeon run (only healing items work there), and not while you're rooted after a gather or by a protector ambush.

Several characters on one account

An account holds up to 6 characters. Switch between them with the character tabs. Your inventory is shared, so any character can use what another one gathered.

  • Move them as a group: there's no multi-select. Instead, invite your own characters to a party ("Invite own character" in the friends panel) and turn on Follow leader in the party frame. Your other idle characters in the same world trail the leader.
  • Fight together: followers within 3 blocks join the leader's side automatically during placement. Turn on Auto-switch fighter in settings and the game selects whichever of your characters has the turn.
  • Craft from one place: the Always craft from setting locks crafting to one character, whichever one you're playing.

Parties

  • Up to 6 characters, from any mix of accounts. The first invite creates the party; anyone can leave, and the leader can kick.
  • The oldest member leads. If the leader leaves, the next oldest takes over.
  • Being in a party doesn't pull you into fights; each member joins on their own. A fight side can be set to Group only so strangers can't join.
  • Run to position walks you to a party member. On the map, party members show in cyan and the leader in gold.
  • Party XP and loot sharing are on Your first fights.

Built-in gathering helper

The game has its own gathering automation panel. Pick a resource and press Start: it walks, searches zones and gathers for you until you press Stop. Every step is still a normal transaction, so it spends gas like playing by hand.

Chat and social

Chat has General, Party and Whisper channels. Right-click a player to message them, add them as a friend, challenge them to a duel or open a trade.
