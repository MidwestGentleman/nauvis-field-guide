Glossary

Words the game uses without explaining.

TermMeaning
APAction Points: what spells and weapon strikes cost. 6 per turn.
MPMovement Points: one per step in a fight. 3 per turn.
ArchimobA rare, named, stronger variant of a mob family (Fukuo, Arakiri, Misunami), with guaranteed rare drops.
City areaThe 3 × 3 zones around a city. Only that city's mobs and resources spawn there.
CrankEnding another player's turn after its 45 seconds run out.
CrushingDestroying gear to get about 25% of its stats back as runes.
GasThe small SUI fee every transaction pays.
JourneyThe beginner checklist, "Surviving Nauvis".
KioskYour on-chain storefront. Your shared inventory and marketplace listings live there.
KolizeumWagered PvP arena.
Line of sightWhether a spell can see its target. Every fighter blocks it, allies included.
MasteryPoints from the daily dungeon quest, spent in the Mastery shop.
Nakwa / Stone / Magma / GaleWater / earth / fire / air variants of a mob.
NauvisThe starting world.
PlacementThe pre-fight phase where you choose a blue starting cell. Each move costs gas.
ProtectorA resource's guardian, met through the 2% gathering ambush. A win has an 80–100% chance to drop a bag of 50.
RankA spell's level, 1 to 6. Ranks cost spell points.
RerollSearching a zone again after 2 hours, which rolls new mobs and resources.
Rune (Ba, Pa, Ra)Small, medium and large stat runes from crushing, used for scribing.
ScribingApplying a rune to gear to change its stats. The rune is always used up.
Star GateThe ring at the world centre where you spawn, and the portal to Yakutia (level 20).
TackleThe Agility check when you try to leave a cell next to an enemy.
YakutiaThe second world, for character level 20+. High-tier resources and no cities.
zkLoginSui's sign-in with Google: your Google login controls a Sui address.
ZoneA 512 × 512-block square of the world with its own mobs and resources.
