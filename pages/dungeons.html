Dungeons and keys

There are three dungeons, one under each Nauvis city. Each is a fixed chain of 6–8 room fights ending in a boss. There's no level gate, but there's no second chance either: lose a room and the run is over.

Rules for every dungeon

  • Every player burns their own key at the portal. A party of four needs four keys.
  • Rooms are fought in order. Between rooms you can only fight the next room or give up; potions that teleport don't work.
  • To fight a room together, everyone must be on the same room number. One player opens the fight, the others join it, and only then does anyone press Ready.
  • Up to 6 players can fight a room.
  • Mob levels in a room are rolled inside each mob's own range, however far the dungeon is from the centre.
  • Lose or give up a room and the run ends. The key is gone. Loot from rooms you already won is kept.
  • Boss rooms can also pay a small SUI bounty, split between the winners, when the game's reward pot is funded.
