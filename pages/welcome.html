What AresRPG is

AresRPG is a turn-based, voxel, Massive Multiplayer Online Role-Playing Game (MMORPG) where you are building your character to conquer dungeons and battle other players in PVP tournaments.

The best part is that every action is a transaction on the Sui blockchain. Which means that you own your character, your items, and your progress, and there is a P2P Marketplace where you can buy and sell items with other players.

You explore the open world of Nauvis, gather, craft, and fight mobs in a grid based mini battle areana that is randomly generated. This guide gives you a lay of the land and helps you get started quickly.

This is an unofficial fan guide. This guide was not written by the AresRPG team athough it does draw on the game's own data wherever possible. Numbers come from the game's public source code, and the data stamp at the bottom says when they were last regenerated. If the game and this page disagree, trust the game and its in-game encyclopedia.

How the game works

The game will feel familiar if you've played a Dofus-style tactics MMO. You walk around an open voxel world in real time. As you move around you will encounter randomly generated mobs that get stronger the further from the city you go. If you interact with one, a fight starts and the game drops you onto a square grid, and you and the mobs take turns. Each turn you spend Action Points (AP) on spells and Movement Points (MP) on steps. Between fights you gather resources, craft gear and food, and level up 11 jobs alongside your character.

The twist is the chain. Your characters, items and pets are objects you own on Sui. Every fight turn, harvest and craft is a small transaction that costs gas, a fraction of a cent of SUI. Nothing is stored on a game server that could be wiped. That also means mistakes stick.

Ten things to know on day one

Read them now, and then the following pages will elaborate on them.

  1. Your class is permanent. So are your name, sex and colours. Read Choosing a class first.
  2. Every action costs gas. That includes each step you take while placing yourself before a fight. Keep a little SUI on the account.
  3. There is no market on the map, No NPC sells tools. You craft your tools in the jobs menu, or buy one from another player on the marketplace menu.
  4. Crafts can fail. A failed craft burns its ingredients but gains experience. So the more you craft the better your odds get. At job level 1 you succeed 50% of the time, so gather extra.
  5. Losing a fight costs nothing but time. You keep your items and XP and drop to 1 HP, which comes back at 1 HP per second.
  6. There is no flee button. Forfeiting a battle gives no XP and no loot for that fight. If in a dungeon you will recieve all the loot from rooms you've beat but will leave the current fight and exit the dungeon.
  7. Thebes is special, you'll spend a good deal of time around here but will need to explore to gain experience. The 9 zones around Thebes only spawn Thebes mobs: Tinkers, Nooks, Brambles and Loritos. Loritos live nowhere else. See regional spawn rules.
  8. Water can't be gathered. It only drops from water mobs like Crabs and Misui Nakwa, and almost every early recipe needs it.
  9. You can't use potions or food in a fight.
  10. A dungeon key is burned when you enter. Lose a room and the run is over. Every party member burns their own key.
