Marketplace and trading

Everything you own is an object on the Sui blockchain, kept in your own kiosk. You can sell it to anyone on the marketplace, or swap directly with a player through the trade window.

Buying

  • The marketplace has Buy, Sell and History tabs. Filter by Equipment, Pets, Runes, Consumables, Resources or Characters, and by level and class, or search by name.
  • Prices are in SUI. The buyer pays a 10% royalty on top of the listed price (at least 0.01 SUI), plus normal network gas.
  • Each item has a price-history chart of daily candles: the open, high, low and close unit price of completed sales, without fees, over 7 days, 30 days, 90 days or a year. Days recorded before candles were added show blank. 24-hour and 30-day volume sit alongside.
  • Characters are sold here too, but an account can't hold more than 6, so the marketplace refuses a 7th.

Selling

  • Set any price you like. Resources, consumables and runes are sold in lots of 1, 10, 100 or 1,000; gear and pets sell one by one.
  • The listing window shows the lowest public price for the same lot size, so a lot of 10 wheat is compared with other lots of 10.
  • The Browse tab hides your own listings, so they look missing. They're under Sell.
  • You can delist at any time. Sale proceeds wait in your kiosk until you press Collect.
  • Listing, buying and collecting are one transaction each. See what costs gas.

The craft planner lists every raw material in a craft, including extra for failed attempts.

Trading with a player

Right-click a player and pick Trade. Both sides put in items, SUI or KARES, and the swap only happens once both accept. The contract holds both offers in escrow, so neither side can take the goods and walk away. Items you receive still pay their royalty, plus gas.

On-chain transfers can't be undone. Only the marketplace and the trade window hold both sides' goods until the deal completes; a direct transfer has no such protection.
