Your first dungeon

Gilded Lorito is the first dungeon and the last chapter of the beginner quests. It has 8 rooms under Thebes and ends with the Golden Lorito. Every player who enters needs their own key.

Getting a key

Every player who enters burns their own key. A team of three needs three keys. You can get one in three ways:

WayHow
Hunt LoritosEach elemental Lorito drops the key 3% of the time. Loritos spawn only in the 9 Thebes zones, nowhere else in the world. See regional spawn rules.
Craft it2 Stone Lorito Plume + 2 Quartz Set Trinket, crafted as Handyman. The plumes need Tailor 10 and the trinkets need Jeweler 10 (plus barley flour, so Farmer 10).
Buy itOther players list keys on the marketplace.

How a run works

  1. Gather at the portal

    The entrance is in Thebes. Get everyone into one party first (up to 6 players).

  2. Everyone enters

    Each player presses F at the portal and burns a key. That starts your own run at room 1. Between rooms you're rooted: you can only fight the next room or give up.

  3. One person opens the room fight

    Exactly one player starts the room's fight, and everyone else joins that same fight during placement. If two people open fights, the party splits and each of you fights the room alone. The opener moves first.

  4. Ready only when everyone is placed

    A player who isn't placed when the fight starts sits out with no turns.

  5. Heal between rooms

    Waiting heals 1 HP per second for free, and food is faster. City and recall potions don't work inside a run.

Losing a room ends the run. The key is gone, and you keep only the loot from rooms you already won. There's no level requirement to enter.

The Golden Lorito

  • Level 10–20, fire, with high Agility (hard to walk away from) and 9 MP. It resists fire and earth a little and is weak to water.
  • Its line attack hits hard, and it can lower your damage for 3 turns.
  • Its loot table is rolled fresh every kill, with no penalty for repeat runs: Golden Feathers and Gilded Talons most of the time, a new key 5%, and the Siluri pet (+80 Wisdom) 1%.

The daily Mastery quest

Once per day, start the quest in the Mastery panel. It assigns one random dungeon. Clear that dungeon's final room and you earn a Mastery point. Points buy resource crates (2), food crates (3), the stat and spell reset scrolls (10 each), pet crates (25) and SUI crates (50). You need that dungeon's key to enter.
