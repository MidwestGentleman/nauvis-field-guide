How you meet mobs

Most mobs simply spawn in packs when a zone is searched. Two kinds only show up by chance: archimobs swap into a normal pack, and protectors jump you while you gather. Bosses wait in dungeons.

The odds are fixed in the game's contracts and don't change with level, Chance or party size. They're per roll, so they add up: a pack of 4 Fuwa-family mobs has about a 4% chance to hold Fukuo, and 50 gathers of the same resource meet its guardian about 64% of the time.

Archimobs

An archimob is a named, stronger member of a mob family. It never spawns by itself: every time a zone rolls its packs, each mob from its family has a 1% chance to be replaced by it. So you find Fukuo wherever Fuwa and Nifuwa roam. Archimobs drop their family's rare parts every time, and two of them drop a dungeon key far more often than their family does.

Searching a zone again after its 2-hour cooldown rerolls every pack, which gives each family member another 1% try.

Protectors

Each of the 33 gatherable resources has its own protector. Protectors never roam. Every gather has a 2% chance to pull its guardian into a solo fight. Your gather still lands first, but you stay rooted until the fight is over and start it with the HP you had when you gathered. Protectors resist their own element and have no weakness. They drop a bag of the resource they guard.

Each resource page also has a Guardian section, for example Green Mushroom.
