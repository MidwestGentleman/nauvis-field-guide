Beginner FAQ

Real questions from a new player's first three weeks, with answers checked against the game's code and data.

Getting started

Where do I spawn? Is it Thebes?

No. You spawn at the Star Gate at the world centre. Thebes is 512 blocks east. The starter mobs and resources are in the 9 zones around Thebes.

The zone around me is empty. Is the game broken?

No, the zone is undiscovered. Press G to search it. That reveals its mobs and resources and costs one transaction.

Character

Can I change my class later?

No. Class, name, sex and colours are permanent. Read Choosing a class first.

I put points in the wrong stat. Can I undo it?

Yes, with a for stat points or a for spell points. Each costs 10 Mastery points, or you can buy one from a player. A scroll applies to the selected character, whose sheet the panels show.

Do AP and MP go up when I level?

No. Every class has 6 AP and 3 MP at every level. Only certain gear and pets add more.

Why can't I equip this item?

Your character level has to be at least the item's level. Listed items can't be equipped either; unlist them first.

Fights

Walking away cost me most of my AP. Why?

That's tackle. Leaving a cell next to an enemy can fail an Agility roll. Casting spells doesn't trigger it.

What do I lose if I die?

Nothing but time and gas. You keep your items and XP and regenerate 1 HP per second. In a dungeon, though, losing ends the run and burns the key.

What does forfeiting do?

It ends the fight as a loss: no XP and no loot. There is no flee.

Can I drink a potion mid-fight?

No. Food and potions only work outside fights.

Does bringing friends mean more loot?

No. Each mob's loot is rolled once and shared round-robin between the winners. Friends do get the party XP bonus.

World

Why can't I find Loritos?

They only spawn in the 9 Thebes zones. See the Lorito restriction.

A monster jumped me while I was gathering.

That's a resource protector, a 2% chance on every gather. Beat it for a bag of exactly 50 of that resource.

Is there fast travel?

Yes. Alchemist potions take you to Thebes, The Ruins or Fuwage (, , ), and a takes you to the Star Gate. City potions only work for cities in the world you're in, and none of these work in a fight, in a dungeon or while you're rooted. Riding a pet (X) makes walking 1.5× faster. See getting around fast.

How do I get to Yakutia?

Reach character level 20, stand within 10 blocks of the Star Gate, press T and pick Yakutia. Each character travels on its own.

The game is slow near The Ruins.

It's a heavy city to draw. Lower graphics quality and render distance before you get close, and play in a single, visible tab.

Economy

My marketplace listings disappeared.

They didn't. The Browse tab hides your own listings.

Who pays the marketplace fee?

The buyer. A 10% royalty (at least 0.01 SUI) goes on top of the listed price. Stacks are listed in lots of 1, 10, 100 or 1,000. See Marketplace and trading.

My SUI went up after I crushed something.

That's Sui's storage deposit coming back when an object is deleted, not a reward.

What is the Kolizeum?

Player-vs-player fights for a real SUI wager. Both sides stake the same amount, the game keeps 10%, and the winner takes the rest. It's entirely optional.
