Create your character

Creating a character takes a minute and costs 1 SUI. Four of the choices can never be changed, so read this page before you click Create.

Permanent: class, name, sex and the three colours. No item, scroll or contract changes them later. A name is also reserved forever: delete the character and nobody, you included, can use that name again.

Pick a class

There are 12 classes. Each has exactly 20 spells: three at level 1, and the rest at levels 3, 6, 9, 13, 17, 21, 26, 31, 36, 42, 48, 54, 60, 70, 80, 90 and 100. Every class starts with the same 6 AP, 3 MP and 55 HP, so what separates them is their spells and which stats they can raise cheaply.

All twelve side by side. The bar under each class shows how many of its damaging spells use each element.

Open any class to read all 20 spells at every rank. Its back link brings you here. For a sortable table, see Choosing a class.

Pick a name

  • 4 to 19 characters.
  • Printable ASCII only: no spaces and no accents. Names are stored in lowercase.
  • The name must be unused, ever. Names of deleted characters stay taken.

What you can change later

Thing Can you undo it?
Stat points (5 per level) Yes, with a Scroll of Rebirth, which refunds every stat point.
Spell points (1 per level) Yes, with a Scroll of Oblivion, which refunds every spell point.
Gear Swap any time outside a fight. You need a character level at least equal to the item's level.
Class, name, sex, colours No.
Deleting the character Allowed, but the 1 SUI isn't refunded and the name stays reserved.

How stats work

Each damage element is scaled by one stat:

Element Stat Also does
earth Strength —
fire Intelligence Boosts your heals
water Chance The winning team's average Chance raises every loot drop chance
air Agility Better crit odds, and harder to get stuck next to enemies (tackle)
— Vitality +1 max HP per point (+2 for Ikari)
— Wisdom +1% fight XP per point, and better at removing or resisting AP/MP loss. It costs 3 points for +1.

A spell deals roll × (100 + stat) / 100, plus any flat Damage bonus. So 100 Strength doubles your earth spells for example.
