Your first hour

Your first character appears at the Star Gate with a checklist called Surviving Nauvis. The checklist tells you what to do; this page tells you where to go and what to fight to get it done.

Where you start

You spawn at the Star Gate, the stone ring at the centre of the world. Your map shows it as 0, 0. Thebes, the first city, is just east, about one zone over. In the city centre, indicated as a gold orb on the map, is the Golden Lorito Dungeon. The 9 zones around Thebes are its city area, indicated on the map with a golden border and only Thebes's own mobs and resources spawn there. Everything on this page is in that area.

  • Moving: click the ground, or click a spot on the map or minimap, and your character walks there. Clicking the map also gets you unstuck.
  • Empty zone? It may not be empty, it could be undiscovered. Stand in it and press G to search it. That costs one transaction and reveals its mobs and resources. A zone can be searched again (rerolled) every 2 hours.

The first chapters

Your character starts with a short list of beginner quests. They tick off on their own as you play, and finishing them all unlocks the game's gathering automation. You don't need to follow the list closely: this is the path it takes you on, and how to tackle it.

  1. First tools: "We all need a hoe."

    Hunt Tinkers and Nooks around Thebes for the hoe's materials and craft it. Then the quests ask for a pickaxe and a sickle, each followed by a first harvest: wheat with the hoe, quartz with the pickaxe, a green mushroom with the sickle. All three tools are cheap Handyman crafts, so hunt for all of them at once: every recipe needs Gnawed Branch from Tinkers. See the hoe and the other two tools below.

  2. Gear up: "Make your own luck."

    Craft or buy a Rootglass Token, a Chance amulet that raises your loot odds. It's a Jeweler craft from quartz and mushrooms, so it uses what you just learned to gather.

  3. Supplies: "Bread before battle."

    Get a Croissant so you can heal between fights instead of waiting. It's a Baker craft from wheat flour and water.

  4. Dungeon and one last secret

    Then your first dungeon, Gilded Lorito, and a final long-term goal. See After the tools.

"We all need a hoe"

Without a hoe you can't farm, and no NPC sells one. You have two options.

Craft it (recommended)

Cheap, and it teaches you hunting, looting and crafting all at once. You need 3 Gnawed Branch + 2 Salvaged Scrap, crafted under Characters → Jobs → Handyman at job level 1.

Buy it from a player

Open the Marketplace and search for Scrap Hoe. Players sell them, and in October 2026 they were listed at 0.5–4 SUI. That's often more than the materials are worth, but it skips about 15 minutes of hunting.

Which mobs to hunt

  • Tinker (level 2–9) drops Gnawed Branch about 58% of the time. Find Tinkers anywhere in the Thebes area, or in any forest on Nauvis.
  • Nook (level 4–10) drops Salvaged Scrap about 67% of the time, usually several at once. Same places as the Tinker: the Thebes area, or forests.
  • Wild Boar also drops Gnawed Branch (70%), but it's level 11–25 and pushes you around. Leave it until you're about level 10.

Brambles aren't in the hoe recipe. Many new players hunt Brambles for it, but the hoe needs Tinkers and Nooks. Brambles drop Rabbit Sinew, which you need for the herbalist's sickle a few steps later, so kill the ones you meet anyway.

A craft can fail. At Handyman level 1, a craft succeeds 50% of the time, and a failure still burns the ingredients. Collect enough for two tries: 6 Gnawed Branch and 4 Salvaged Scrap.

How to craft it

  1. Open Characters, pick your character, and open the Jobs tab.
  2. Open the Jobs menu and choose Handyman and find Scrap Hoe in the recipe list.
  3. Craft. Win or lose, you get Handyman XP.
  4. Equip the hoe in your tool slot. Gathering needs the right tool equipped, not just carried.

The other two starter tools

Both are Handyman level-1 crafts, so they work exactly like the hoe:

Scrap Pickaxe

Tin-Edge Sickle

Gnawed Branch is in all three recipes, so one long Tinker hunt covers 9 branches for the set (18 to allow for failed crafts). The quests ask for them in this order: hoe, pickaxe, sickle.

Tool Job it unlocks Recipe Hunt
Scrap Pickaxe Miner (quartz) 3 Gnawed Branch + 2 Beak Shard Tinkers, plus Loritos for Beak Shard (29–44%). Loritos spawn only in the Thebes area.
Tin-Edge Sickle Herbalist (mushrooms) 3 Gnawed Branch + 2 Rabbit Sinew Tinkers, plus Brambles for Rabbit Sinew (32%).

So the four mobs to hunt early are Tinker, Nook, Bramble and Lorito, and all four live around Thebes. At level 1–5 they come alone or in pairs, which suits a new character.

After the tools

  • Wheat, quartz, green mushroom: all three grow in the Thebes area. See Gathering, jobs and crafting.
  • Rootglass Token, a Chance amulet: 1 Mushroom Lacquer + 10 Quartz Powder (Jeweler). See its item page for the full chain.
  • Croissant: 2 Wheat Flour + 1 Water (Baker). It heals 10 HP between fights.
  • Gilded Lorito, your first dungeon. See Your first dungeon.
  • Suize, the secret last step: a level-50 Farmer resource that only grows in the second world, Yakutia, which needs character level 20. Treat it as a long-term goal.

Stock up on Water. You can't gather Water; it only drops from mobs. Almost every powder, flour and potion needs it. Water Loritos (Lorito Nakwa) around Thebes drop 10–20 at a time, 70% of the time.
